-Access detailed rules
1. In this type of visit, you will definitely get standard or elite weapons every 10 visits. The basic probability of standard weapons is <color=#AC65E8>[VAL5]</color> (including the guaranteed comprehensive probability <color=#AC65E8>[ VAL6]</color>), the basic probability of elite weapons <color=#EDA51C>[VAL7]</color> (including the guaranteed comprehensive probability <color=#EDA51C>[VAL8]</color>).

Starting from the next visit, the probability will increase <color=#EDA51C>[VAL9]</color>, and you will definitely obtain an elite weapon every 70 visits. When you obtain an elite weapon at any time, the count will be reset, and the probability of obtaining an elite weapon will return to the basic probability. If the elite weapon obtained during this visit is not an elite weapon with an increased probability in this period, the elite weapon obtained next time must be an elite weapon with an increased probability in this period.
3. The guaranteed count for this type of access is not cumulative and is independent of the guaranteed count for other types of access and does not affect each other.
4. In this type of visit, every time you get an old weapon, you will get 2 additional extraction vouchers; every time you get a standard humanoid or weapon, you will get an additional 12 redundant fragments; every time you get an elite humanoid or weapon, you will get an additional 60 redundant fragments.
